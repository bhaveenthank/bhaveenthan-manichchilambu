# Data licence

The data made in this project is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

This covers:
- the hand records and their English and Tamil glosses (`data/seeds/`)
- the entity list and the graph (`data/graph/`, `web/public/data/`)
- the review sheets and evaluation material (`annotation/`, `evaluation/`)

You may share and adapt it for any purpose, as long as you give credit. A suggested credit, and the citation for any single record, is on each record's page on the site.

**Machine output.** The line notes and the unchecked machine drafts (`data/candidates/`) were written by a language model and haven't been checked. They're shared under the same licence, but they're marked as machine output everywhere they appear, and they shouldn't be cited as readings of the text.

**Not covered: the Tamil text.** The text of Silappatikāram and Maṇimēkalai comes from Project Madurai (<https://www.projectmadurai.org/>) and stays under their terms. The original files, with their header pages intact, are in `data/raw/`. Keep those headers if you redistribute the text.
