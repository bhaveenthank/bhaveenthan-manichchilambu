# ACL Rolling Review (ARR) / *ACL 2026–27 long paper guidelines

- Template: official ACL style (`acl.sty`, `acl_natbib.bst`), `\usepackage[review]{acl}` for submission.
- Long paper: **at most 8 pages** of main content. References are unlimited and do not count.
- A **"Limitations"** section (unnumbered, `\section*{Limitations}`) is **mandatory**, placed after the conclusion and before references; it does not count toward the page limit.
- An "Ethics Statement" / "Ethical Considerations" section is optional, also not counted.
- Appendices go after the references and do not count; the paper must be understandable without them.
- Double-blind: no author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, or links that reveal identity (no personal GitHub URLs). Self-citation in third person only.
- Paper size A4, two columns (handled by the style). Do not change margins, fonts or spacing.
- Non-Latin script (Tamil) may be typeset with XeLaTeX/LuaLaTeX + fontspec; the ACL template allows this.
- Tables: booktabs style recommended. Figures must be legible in greyscale.
- Citation style: natbib, `\citet{}` for textual, `\citep{}` for parenthetical.
